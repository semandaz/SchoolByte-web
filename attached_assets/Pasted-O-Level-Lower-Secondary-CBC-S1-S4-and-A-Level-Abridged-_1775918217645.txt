1. PHYSICS (Compulsory O-Level; Principal A-Level)
O-Level CBC (S1–S4) – Programme Planner
S1 (108 periods total)

Term 1: Introduction to Physics (6); Mechanics & properties of matter – Measurements in Physics (30)
Term 2: Mechanics & properties of matter – States of matter (10); Effects of forces (16); Heat – Temperature measurements (10)
Term 3: Heat – Heat transfer (12); Expansion of solids/liquids/gases (8); Light – Nature of light; reflection at plane surfaces (16)

S2 (108 periods)

Term 1: Mechanics & properties of matter – Work, energy & power (20); Turning effect of forces, centre of gravity & stability (16)
Term 2: Mechanics & properties of matter – Pressure in solids/fluids (16); Mechanical properties of materials & Hooke’s law (12); Light – Reflection at curved surfaces (8)
Term 3: Magnetism – Magnets & magnetic fields (10); Electricity – Electrostatics (8); Earth & space physics – The solar system (18)

S3 (144 periods)

Term 1: Mechanics & properties of matter – Linear & non-linear motion (30); Light – Refraction, dispersion & colour (18)
Term 2: Light – Lenses & optical instruments (18); Waves – General wave properties (16); Sound waves (14)
Term 3: Heat – Heat quantities & vapours (20); Earth & space physics – Stars & galaxies (14); Satellites & communication (14)

S4 (136 periods)

Term 1: Electricity – Introduction to current electricity (14); Voltage, resistance & Ohm’s law (18); Magnetism – Electromagnetic effects (16)
Term 2: Electricity – Electric energy distribution & consumption (28); Modern physics – Atomic models (20)
Term 3: Modern physics – Nuclear processes (20); Digital electronics (20)

A-Level Abridged 2025 (S5–S6 – 9 periods/week)
S5 (324 periods total)

Term 1: Measurement & dimensions of physical quantities (incl. dimensional analysis); Statics (resultant force, moments); Linear motion (equations, relative, momentum, Newton’s laws); Motion under gravity (free fall, projectiles); Work, energy & power; Solid friction (static/dynamic)
Term 2: Fluid mechanics (molecular forces, pressure/density, flow); Mechanical properties of matter (Hooke’s law, modulus); Thermometry (scales, thermometers); Heat quantities (capacity, latent heat); Transfer of heat (conduction, convection, radiation); Behaviour of gases (ideal, kinetic theory, real, vapour pressure); Thermodynamics (internal energy, first law)
Term 3: Reflection of light (plane/curved); Refraction of light (plane, critical angle, lenses); Optical instruments (microscopes, telescopes); Electrostatics (charge production, force, field); Capacitors (parallel plate, charging/discharging, energy); Digital electronics (junction diodes, transistors)

S6 (324 periods total)

Term 1: Circular motion (centripetal force, conical pendulum, tracks); Simple harmonic motion (displacement/velocity/energy); Gravitation (laws, orbits, satellites); Progressive waves (properties, interference, diffraction, polarisation); Stationary waves (formation, pipes/strings, resonance); Sound waves (production, beats, Doppler)
Term 2: Current electricity (Ohm’s law, meters); Magnetism in matter (fields, magnetisation); Magnetic effect of electric current (force on conductor, Biot-Savart, torque); Electromagnetic induction (laws, self/mutual); A.C. circuits (measurement, inductance/impedance, power)
Term 3: Atomic particles (Rutherford model, cathode/positive rays, specific charge); Quantum theory; Nuclear processes

2. BIOLOGY (Compulsory O-Level; Principal A-Level)
O-Level CBC (S1–S4) – 12 spiral themes
S1 (108 periods) – Diversity of Living Things

Term 1: Introduction to Biology (8); Cells (20); Classification (8)
Term 2: Classification (36 – Five Kingdoms + Viruses)
Term 3: Insects (20); Flowering plants (16)

S2 (108 periods)

Term 1 (Soil): Physical/chemical properties (20); Erosion & conservation (16)
Term 2 (Nutrition): Types & nutrient compounds (11); In green plants (10); In mammals (15)
Term 3 (Transport): In plants (18); In animals (18)

S3 (144 periods)

Term 1: Respiration – Gaseous exchange (14); Aerobic/anaerobic (15); Excretion in animals (19)
Term 2: Coordination – Chemical in humans (18); Nervous (22); Receptor organs (8)
Term 3: Locomotion in mammals (20); Growth & development (growth 12; development 16)

S4 (134 periods)

Term 1 (Reproduction): Asexual in plants (10); Sexual in plants (16); Sexual in humans (22)
Term 2: Genetics – Inheritance (20); Variation & selection (13); Ecology – Concept (3); Food chains/webs (12)
Term 3: Associations in communities (18); Humans & natural environment (20)

A-Level Abridged 2025 (S5–S6)
S5

Term 1: Cell Biology (90 – chemicals of life, microscopy, ultrastructure, tissues)
Term 2: Nutrition in plants (28 – C3/C4 photosynthesis); Transport in humans (80 – gases, immunity)
Term 3: Respiration (30 – mitochondrion, ATP); Homeostasis (78 – negative feedback, osmoregulation)

S6

Term 1: Coordination (90 – plant hormones, impulse transmission, receptors, behaviour)
Term 2: Inheritance & evolution (90 – nucleic acids 18, gene technology 72, Mendelian/non-Mendelian, evolutionary advancements, speciation/extinction); Growth in plants & development in insects (36)
Term 3: Ecology (90 – population, succession/restoration, energy flow, carbon footprint, invasive species, food security)

S2: Indices/surds, transformations/vectors, graphs, sets, inequalities, mappings/functions.
S3: Trigonometry I/II, vectors/matrices, probability, straight-line equations, quadratics, business math.
S4: Composite functions, linear programming, loci, 3D geometry (revision focus).

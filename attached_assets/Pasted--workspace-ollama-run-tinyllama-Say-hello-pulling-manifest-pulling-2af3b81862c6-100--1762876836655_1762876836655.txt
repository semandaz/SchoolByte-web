~/workspace$ ollama run tinyllama "What is biology?"
Biology, or the study of living organisms, is a branch of biology that focuses on understanding and 
studying the principles that govern life at all levels of organization from genetic to ecological 
scales. Biochemistry, genetics, evolutionary biology, and physiological systems are other important 
aspects of biology that fall under this broader discipline. Biologists study organisms at different 
levels of organization, including their anatomy, physiology, behavior, and adaptation to their 
environment.

Biochemistry is a field that focuses on the chemical basis for biological processes, which include 
enzyme mechanisms, protein chemistry, and cellular metabolism. Genetics examines how genetic 
information is transmitted from one generation to another and how it is encoded in the DNA. 
Evolutionary biology looks at how species evolve over time, including the mechanisms of natural 
selection and adaptation. Physiological systems are those that govern the fundamental physical 
processes of life, such as respiration, circulation, digestion, sensory perception, and movement.

Biochemistry and genetics help to understand cellular processes such as cell division, growth, 
differentiation, and aging. Evolutionary biology contributes to our understanding of how species 
evolve and adapt to their environments. Physiological systems are important in developing treatments 
for diseases caused by genetic mutations or environmental stressors.

In summary, biology is the study of living organisms as a whole and includes the various disciplines 
that study living beings at different levels.

3. Syllabus Construct + Themes/Topics Progression (S1–S4 Examples)
All subjects use the same 4-column format above. Themes spiral with increasing depth. Here are core subjects with official programme planners (extracted from NCDC PDFs). For electives or less common ones, follow the same pattern from their specific syllabus on ncdc.go.ug.
Mathematics (Compulsory all years – 5 periods/week S1–S2, 6 S3–S4)
Main Themes (spiral S1–S4): Numbers; Geometry & Measures; Data & Probability; Patterns & Algebra.

S1 (180 periods): Foundations – Number bases, integers, fractions/decimals/percentages, geometric constructions, bearings, data collection, sequences, basic equations.
S2 (180 periods): Abstraction – Indices/surds, transformations (reflection/rotation), graphs, set theory, inequalities, mappings/functions.
S3 (180 periods): Application – Trigonometry I/II, vectors, matrices, probability, straight-line equations, quadratics, business math.
S4 (100+ periods revision): Optimisation – Composite functions, linear programming, 3D geometry/loci.

Example Competency (S2 Patterns & Algebra – Mappings): The learner understands and uses arrow diagrams/mappings to represent relations and functions.
LOs: Use arrow diagrams (k/u); identify domain/range (k/u); distinguish function vs non-function (u/s).
Activities: Real-life Ugandan examples (e.g., market pricing functions, family relation mappings).
Assessment: Group project mapping a small business supply chain.
Biology (Compulsory – 3/4 periods/week)
Main Themes (12 themes spiral): Diversity of Living Things; Soil; Nutrition; Transport; Respiration; Excretion; Coordination; Locomotion; Growth & Development; Reproduction; Genetics; Inter-relationships.

S1 (108 periods): Diversity – Introduction, cells, classification, insects, flowering plants.
S2 (108 periods): Soil; Nutrition in plants/animals; Transport in plants/animals.
S3 (144 periods): Respiration; Excretion; Coordination (chemical/nervous); Locomotion; Growth & development.
S4 (134 periods): Reproduction (asexual/sexual); Genetics (inheritance/variation); Inter-relationships (ecology, food chains, human impact).

Example Competency (S4 Genetics – Inheritance): The learner appreciates that transmission of characteristics is from parents to offspring through heredity.
LOs: Understand cell division/meiosis (k/u); explain sex determination (u/s).
Activities: Investigate local crop/livestock breeding (Ugandan agriculture context).
Assessment: Project on genetic disorders in Ugandan communities + conservation strategies.
(Other core subjects follow identically – e.g., English: communication, literature, functional writing, media; History & Political Education: Understanding our past, East Africa/Uganda, global issues, democracy/human rights; Geography: environment/resources, human settlements, sustainability + fieldwork; Chemistry/Physics: scientific inquiry, matter/energy, forces – heavy practicals. Full planners in their PDFs.)
Electives (e.g., Agriculture, ICT, Art & Design): More vocational – themes focus on practical projects/products for UVQF Level 1.
